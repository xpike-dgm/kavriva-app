## Meaning, qualifications and HOLD

“Never sole AI authority” controls the final decision or effect. It does not prohibit all approved assistance: Decision5 still permits approved-content explanation, already-eligible candidate ranking, drafting and bounded route/tool proposals. Such help never supplies missing canonical technical truth, current applicability, safety, source verification, publication, privileged policy, protected audit, release, legal/privacy, money or retention authority. Preserve unsupported, final, definitive high-risk, outside approved guide/state and high-impact exactly; replacing the qualified categories with broader bans or narrower permissions would change the source.

If a category, source, applicability, verification, current owning authority or required evidence is ambiguous or missing, HOLD the affected decision/effect. Do not guess which side of the boundary an action occupies. No invented certainty or fallback answer authorizes it; confidence, valid output shape, previous approval or agreement between models cannot remove HOLD. This reference records the required HOLD behavior without pretending to implement T-E9-010's separate detailed rule or a runtime integration.

E9 proposes; E3 verifies from current canonical sources; E1 renders the owning result. Motorcycle/variant, applicable approved guide, prerequisites, readiness/safety and provenance remain independently required. Model output, caller ALLOW and a reference-review PASS are never their replacement. Decision4 keeps retrieved/web/community/document/media/tool content untrusted and tool/action authority outside model output. No private imports, public contract changes or E1–E9 cycle.

Project/company work follows Decision7 and DEC-0028/DEC-0029: separate implementer, reviewer and validator roles, evidence and independent review. AI never impersonates legal identity, store ownership or qualified attestation. [Decision7 source](https://github.com/xpike-dgm/motobakim-plan/blob/fa914f013fdcd032faed876689092da245989459/05_ADR/RECORDS/ADR-014__AI_LLM_ASSISTANCE_AND_DECISION_LAYER.md#L87-L88).
